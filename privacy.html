Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy covers ddotnollid.com and the tools Ddot Nollid Productions uses to make and publish Ddot Nollid's music and videos. Ddot Nollid is the music of Todd Dillon.

The website

ddotnollid.com doesn't use analytics or ad trackers, and it doesn't ask you to sign in. Our host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) for security and reliability. Links to streaming services, video platforms and stores take you to those services, whose own privacy policies apply.

Our mailing list

If you join the mailing list, your email address is stored by our email provider, Kit, and used only to send Ddot Nollid news and new music. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

When you email us

If you write to hello@ddotnollid.com (for a custom song, a collaboration or a licensing request), we use your name, email address and what you send us only to reply and to deliver what you asked for. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else, except as needed to deliver your order or when the law requires it. Ask us any time to delete it.

Ddot Nollid Studio (our production tool)

Ddot Nollid Studio is a private tool used only by Ddot Nollid Productions. It connects to our own Google Drive to read raw video we upload there and to save finished videos back. It accesses only our own account's files, uses them only to edit and publish Ddot Nollid videos, and doesn't collect, share or sell data about anyone else. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Children

This site isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions: hello@ddotnollid.com.
