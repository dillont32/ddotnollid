    The story

    Read the name backwards and you’ll find the man behind the chrome. Ddot Nollid is Todd Dillon turned up to ten: a smiling cyborg in a fur-lined hood, braver than the man, more persistent, and always glad to meet someone new.

    I’m not here to boast. The best songs I know started with other people: a friend’s poem, a card table, a Michigan river, the Monday you needed a cold one. Your story belongs here too.

    I believe we survive united and thrive united, and that we can’t do either while we’re divided. So if a song here makes you think of someone, send it their way. That’s the whole mission: bringing people together, one song at a time.
