    The story

    Read the name backwards and you’ll find the man behind the chrome. Ddot Nollid is the persona: a smiling cyborg in a fur-lined hood with headphones on, built to carry real stories a long way from home.

    The songs come from garages and card tables, Michigan rivers and beach sunsets, friends’ poems and the Monday you needed a cold one. Each cover is a world of its own, and every one of them is wearing the same grin.

    • They Don’t UnderstandInspired by the life and poetry of Tony Hernandez.
    • Palm Tree DreamsInspired by the words of T. Craig Goodwin and the spirit of Corky Celeski.
